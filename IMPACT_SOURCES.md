# How much money Americans lose to expiring benefits (sources)

Every figure below is an annual, nationwide estimate from a primary or official source, for a category Benefit Borough tracks. Links were checked on Oct 24–25, 2026. The years differ, so the total is a combined estimate, not a single measurement.

| What's lost each year | Amount | Data year | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid time off forfeited (236 million days) | $65.5B | 2018 | U.S. Travel Association with Oxford Economics and Ipsos, ["A Record 768 Million U.S. Vacation Days Went Unused in '18"](https://www.ustravel.org/press/study-record-768-million-us-vacation-days-went-unused-18-opportunity-cost-billions) |
| 401(k) employer match left unclaimed | $24B | 2014 | Financial Engines, ["Missing Out"](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20150512005336/en/) (4.4M employees, 553 companies; the authors call it an undercount) |
| Education tax credits not claimed (AOTC/LLC) | $6.3B | TY2022 | IRS Statistics of Income, [Credits and Deductions Gap Summary (Dec 2024)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/24rpcreditdeductiongapsummary.pdf) |
| Overdraft and NSF fees paid | $5.8B | 2023 | CFPB, [Overdraft/NSF data spotlight (Apr 2024)](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_overdraft-nsf_data-spotlight_2024-04.pdf) |
| Deferred interest charged retroactively on store-card promos | $3.8B | 2024 | CFPB, [Consumer Credit Card Market Report 2025](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-credit-card-market-report_2025.pdf) |
| Credit card rewards forfeited | $0.5B | ~2022 | CFPB, [Consumer Credit Card Market Report 2023](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-credit-card-market-report_2023.pdf) |
| **Total** | **≈ $105.9B** | | |

Additional verified figures that are not in the total, because Benefit Borough doesn't track them yet:
- Earned Income Tax Credit unclaimed: $5.9B (IRS, TY2020, same IRS report).
- Child Tax Credit unclaimed: $9.3B (IRS, TY2020, same IRS report).
- Credit card late fees: $17.0B (CFPB 2025 report, 2024 data).

Real problems with no reputable national dollar figure (described with percentages or per-person averages instead):
- **Health FSAs:** about half of account holders forfeit money, $436 on average (EBRI, 2023), [EBRI FSA database](https://www.ebri.org/content/updates-from-ebri-s-flexible-spending-account-database).
- **401(k) vesting:** forfeitures occur in 30% of job separations, averaging 40% of the departing worker's balance (Vanguard, 2025).
- **ESPPs:** only about 30% of eligible employees participate, leaving about $3,079 a year on the table on average (Babenko & Sen, *Review of Financial Studies*, 2014).
- **Pell Grants:** the high school class of 2024 alone left an estimated $4.4B unclaimed by not filing the FAFSA (NCAN, Apr 2025). This is one class, not an annual total.
- **Income-driven repayment:** more than half of borrowers didn't recertify on time (Dept. of Education data via Brookings).
- **Dental:** only 5% of enrollees reach their annual maximum (National Association of Dental Plans, a trade group).

Not counted, because they aren't annual losses: $27B in unused gift card balances (Bankrate 2024) and the gap between card rewards earned and redeemed (mostly points people still hold).
