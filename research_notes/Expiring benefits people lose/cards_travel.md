# Credit card credits, travel/loyalty perks and memberships that expire or reset (US, as of Oct 2026)

Note on sourcing: most sources are third-party points/miles blogs and personal-finance outlets (NerdWallet, TPG, Frequent Miler, AwardWallet, View from the Wing, Motley Fool, OMAAT). Issuer pages were not fetched directly (NerdWallet returned 403). The report writer should frame dollar amounts as "per issuer terms as reported by X" and tell readers to confirm in the issuer app's benefits tracker.

## 1. Premium card coupon credits (2026): what resets, cadence, value

### Takeaway
Premium cards now split most of their value into small monthly, quarterly or semiannual "use-it-or-lose-it" buckets, most of them on a calendar year (resetting Jan 1, Apr 1, Jul 1, Oct 1 or on the 1st of each month), not the cardmember year. Two big changes: the Amex Platinum refresh (fee to $895 from Sept 18, 2025 for new applicants and from the first renewal on or after Jan 2, 2026 for existing cardholders) and the 2025 Chase Sapphire Reserve refresh. Missing a single period loses that slice for good. Where to check: the issuer app's benefits/credit tracker (Amex "Benefits" tab, Chase "Card benefits" tracker), monthly statements (look for statement-credit lines), and enrollment pages.

### Inferences
- Cadence types to catalog: monthly (Uber Cash, digital entertainment, Walmart+, Dunkin', Gold dining, DoorDash, Lyft, Peloton), quarterly (Platinum Resy, lululemon), semiannual (Platinum hotel, Gold Resy, CSR Edit/dining/StubHub, Blacklane), calendar-year annual (airline fee, CLEAR, Uber One, Strata hotel/Splurge), cardmember-year (Venture X travel credit, co-brand free nights and companion certificates), multi-year (Global Entry/TSA PreCheck every 4-5 years).
- The highest-risk lapses are the reset deadlines that fall mid-year: the last day of each quarter (Mar 31, Jun 30, Sep 30, Dec 31) for quarterly credits, and Jun 30 and Dec 31 for semiannual credits. Many sites publish "expiring credits" reminders around these dates — e.g. [Upgraded Points Q2 2025](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-platinum-q2-2025-expiring-credits)
- Fixes: enroll (Amex requires enrollment for Resy, lululemon, digital entertainment and others), add the card as the payment method in the Uber, Walmart+ and DoorDash apps, set calendar reminders for the last week of each period, and check the benefit tracker in the issuer app.

## 2. Hotel free-night certificates, companion passes, lounge/upgrade certificates, elite deadlines

### Takeaway
Co-brand hotel free-night certificates generally expire 12 months after they are issued. Hilton sometimes grants a 1-2 month extension by phone; Marriott ended extensions in Dec 2023; Hyatt certificates must be checked out before the expiration date. Airline companion certificates are issued at card renewal and carry a travel-by date. Where to check: the loyalty account "Certificates/Awards" page, and the card renewal email.

### Cited Findings
- Hilton: certificates generally expire 12 months after issue and the stay must be completed by that date. Members report 1-2 month extensions, though agents are inconsistent — [View from the Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/hilton-agents-say-free-night-extensions-stop-at-2-weeks-members-are-still-getting-2-months/); [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/got-an-expiring-hilton-free-night-certificate-call-and-ask-for-an-extension/)
- Marriott: certificates expire one year after issue, and extensions were reportedly ended on 12/5/2023 — [YMMV](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2023/12/06/marriotts-decision-to-eliminate-extension-of-free-nights-was-it-a-mistake/); [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/marriott-rules-for-extending-free-nights/)
- Hyatt: the reservation's checkout date must be before the certificate's expiration date, and Hyatt's site may keep showing expired certificates. Promotional certificates (e.g. Hyatt Go) can be as short as about 4 months — [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/when-do-hyatt-certs-expire-perhaps-a-day-earlier-than-you-think/); [Travel Arbitrage](https://www.travelarbitrage.net/en/blog/hyatt-go-free-nights-certificates-2026)
- General: a one-time extension of a few months is sometimes possible but unlikely to be granted twice. Use certificates before points — [TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/hotel-free-night-certificates-before-points)
- Delta Reserve companion certificate: issued at renewal starting year 2; travel must be completed by the expiry — [OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/delta-reserve-credit-card-review/)

### Inferences
- The fixes are to book (or, for Hilton, travel) before the expiration date, or to call for an extension (Hilton only). Booking a refundable stay to "lock in" a certificate may not work, since many programs require the stay itself to be completed by the expiry.

### Gaps
- IHG certificate rules, Alaska Atmos companion fare expiry, United/AA upgrade certificates, Delta Global/Regional Upgrade Certificates, and lounge day-pass expiry (e.g. United Club passes) were not found in this session.
- Elite status qualification deadlines (Delta MQD year, AA Loyalty Point year Mar–Feb, Hyatt/Hilton calendar year) were not researched with sources — gap.

## 3. Points/miles expiration by program

### Takeaway
Most major US airline currencies no longer expire: Delta, United, Southwest, JetBlue and Alaska Atmos. American (24 months of inactivity) and Frontier (12 months) are the exceptions. Hotel points mostly expire after inactivity: IHG 12 months, Choice 18 months, Wyndham 18 months of inactivity plus a hard 4-year limit, and Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt 24 months. Any earning, redeeming or transfer usually resets the clock. Where to check: the loyalty account page ("points expire on" field), statements and emails.

### Cited Findings
- American AAdvantage: 24 months of inactivity. Earning, redeeming or a transfer from Citi resets it. Members under 21 and AA co-brand cardholders are exempt — [Frequent Miler 2026](https://frequentmiler.com/how-to-keep-points-and-miles-alive/)
- Delta, United, Southwest, JetBlue, Alaska Atmos: no expiration — [Frequent Miler 2026](https://frequentmiler.com/how-to-keep-points-and-miles-alive/); [Travel Freely](https://travelfreely.com/when-do-airline-miles-and-hotel-points-expire-and-how-do-you-keep-them-alive/)
- Frontier: 12 months of inactivity — [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/how-to-keep-points-and-miles-alive/)
- Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt: 24 months of inactivity (holding a Hyatt card counts as activity). IHG: 12 months, elites exempt; reports conflict on whether a transfer resets it. Choice: 18 months, Gold+ elites exempt. Wyndham: 18 months of inactivity, and points also expire 4 years after they were earned regardless of activity — [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/how-to-keep-points-and-miles-alive/)
- Aeroplan (Canadian but popular in the US as a transfer partner): expiry paused until Nov 30, 2026; then 18 months of inactivity — [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2025/11/04/air-canada-extends-pause-on-aeroplan-points-expiry-until-late-2026/); [TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/news/aeroplan-resumes-points-expiration)
- 45.1% of travel cardholders in a 1,000-person survey say they have let points or miles expire unused (commercial survey, directional) — [BestMoney](https://www.bestmoney.com/credit-cards/learn-more/travel-credit-card-rewards-survey)

### Inferences
- Bank transferable currencies (Amex MR, Chase UR, Citi TY, Capital One) don't expire while the account is open but can be forfeited if the card is closed. This is from general knowledge, not a fetched source; verify it.
- The easy fix is a small purchase through a shopping portal or a co-brand card swipe to reset the clock.

### Gaps
- Hawaiian (merged into Atmos), Spirit Free Spirit (inactivity rule; Spirit went through bankruptcy in 2025) and Breeze not sourced. Bank-currency forfeiture-on-closure terms not sourced.

## 4. Airline flight credits/vouchers and cruise credits

### Takeaway
Flight credits are the biggest pool of easy-to-lose travel value. Most expire 12 months from the original ticket date, not the cancellation date. Southwest reversed its "credits never expire" policy for bookings made on or after May 28, 2025. Some credits must be booked by the date, others require travel to be completed by it. Where to check: the airline account wallet or "trip credits" page, and cancellation confirmation emails (which show the credit or ticket number).

### Cited Findings
- American: Trip Credit lasts 12 months for AAdvantage members and 6 months for non-members (issued on or after Apr 2, 2024), book-by. Flight Credit lasts 1 year from the original ticket, travel-by — [Autopilot, Aug 2026](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared)
- United: Future Flight Credit about 12 months from the original ticket (travel-by). Electronic Travel Certificate about 12 months (book-by). TravelBank cash 5 years from each deposit — [Autopilot](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared)
- Delta: eCredit lasts 1 year from the original ticket for voluntary cancellations, or 5 years if Delta caused the cancellation — [Autopilot](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared)
- Southwest: bookings made on or after May 28, 2025 produce credits that expire (Basic fare 6 months, Choice tiers 12 months, from the original booking date, travel-by). Credits from bookings made on or before May 27, 2025 still never expire — [Autopilot](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared); [Travel Market Report](https://travelmarketreport.com/air/articles/southwest-airlines-will-enforce-its-new-flight-credit-expiration-policy-this-spring); earlier no-expiry policy: [Afar](https://www.afar.com/magazine/southwest-flight-credits-will-no-longer-expire-ever)
- JetBlue: 12 months from the original ticket, book-by. Alaska: 12 months from the original ticket or 30 days from cancellation, whichever is later, book-by — [Autopilot](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared)
- Across carriers, credit lifetimes range from 30 days to 5 years, and the clock usually starts at the original booking date — [Autopilot](https://withautopilot.com/blog/airline-flight-credits-compared)

### Gaps
- Cruise future cruise credits (FCC) expiry was not researched — gap. Spirit, Frontier and Hawaiian credit rules not found.

## 5. Membership perks (Costco, Prime, Walmart+, DashPass)

### Takeaway
The Costco Executive 2% reward is capped at $1,250 a year and arrives as a certificate with the renewal notice. Costco's own pages conflict on whether it expires. Downgrading or cancelling with a refund forfeits it. Walmart+ and DashPass themselves are often "free" through card credits (Amex Platinum Walmart+ monthly, CSR DashPass), which lapse if the card isn't set as the payment method.

### Cited Findings
- Costco Executive: 2% reward capped at $1,250 per 12 months. Only the primary and household cardholders' purchases count. Members who downgrade or cancel with a refund get no reward. One Costco page says the reward "never expires" — [Costco Executive Rewards](https://www.costco.com/executive-rewards.html); [Costco membership benefits](https://www.costco.com/my-life-executive-membership-benefits.html)
- Amex Platinum covers Walmart+ at up to $12.95 a month — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/how-the-american-express-platinum-card-can-pay-for-its-annual-fee-3-times-over-in-2026); CSR includes DashPass plus monthly DoorDash credits — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/news/chase-sapphire-reserve-150k-offer-april-2026)

### Gaps
- Amazon Prime perks that expire (e.g. Prime Day credits, promotional Amazon credits with expiry, "no-rush shipping" digital credits) were not sourced. Walmart+ perk specifics (Paramount+ or Peacock choice, fuel discounts) not sourced. The Costco certificate printed expiry is unresolved (the US certificate historically stated an expiry around end of the following year; verify).

## 6. Breakage/usage statistics

### Takeaway
There is no solid public statistic on what share of premium-card coupon credits go unused; the "25-40% breakage" figures that circulate are unsourced. The best citable numbers are on rewards in general: 23% of cardholders redeemed no rewards in the past year (CreditCards.com, 2025), down from 31% (Bankrate, 2021); 45% of travel cardholders have let points or miles expire (BestMoney); J.D. Power found only 36% of cardholders understand all their benefits.

### Cited Findings
- 23% of rewards cardholders didn't redeem rewards in the past year, compared with 31% in Bankrate's January 2021 survey (the authors caution the two aren't directly comparable) — [CreditCards.com](https://www.creditcards.com/statistics/unused-credit-card-rewards-poll/); [AOL](https://www.aol.com/finance/probably-sitting-hundreds-dollars-credit-160518405.html)
- 31% of cardholders never redeem points annually (Bankrate Money Pulse, 2017) — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2017/05/17/1113337/0/en/Billions-in-Breakage-How-31-of-Credit-Cardholders-Can-Take-Charge-of-their-Unused-Rewards-and-Do-Some-Good-in-the-Process.html)
- 45.1% of travel cardholders have let points or miles expire. CFPB-cited figure: more than $47B in rewards earned a year, with tens of billions unredeemed — [BestMoney](https://www.bestmoney.com/credit-cards/learn-more/travel-credit-card-rewards-survey)
- J.D. Power: cardholders average 16 benefits, but only about 36% say they understand all of them. Those who use benefits spend about $400 a month more on the card — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/credit-card-users-benefits-JD-power-regional-bank/561594/)
- A ShopBack post claims monthly-split credits see "25-40% breakage", but it cites no study, so it is unreliable — [ShopBack](https://www.shopback.com/blog/finance/is-amex-platinum-still-worth-it-2026)
- Credits for merchants people don't already use (Equinox, lululemon) are the ones most often skipped (qualitative) — [InsideFlyer](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/amex-platinum-cardholders-may-miss-key-benefits/)

### Inferences
- Issuers deliberately split credits into small periods; the industry assumes breakage, but none of the sources found quantify it. The writer should present breakage as plausible but unmeasured, and lean on the rewards-redemption and benefit-awareness stats.

### Gaps
- No issuer disclosure (e.g. Amex 10-K remarks on credit utilization) or independent study of coupon-credit utilization was found. No JD Power 2025-2026 US data on the benefits dimension.
