# Tax, Government-Program and Student Benefits That Expire or Are Deadline-Bound (US, as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-24. Budget-limited pass (~17 tool calls). Many findings come from secondary sources (university aid offices, trade press) summarizing primary rules; flagged where relevant. "Data source" lines name the document that would hold the information a consumer or app would need. They are inferences from how each program works, not cited facts, unless a source is given.

## Tax: refund window, refundable credits, retirement/HSA deadlines, energy credits ended by the 2025 law

### Takeaway
The biggest "use it or lose it" tax item is the 3-year window to claim a refund. About $1.2B in tax-year-2022 refunds (1.3M+ people, median $686) went unclaimed as of the April 15, 2026 deadline. The next window to close is tax year 2023, by about April 15, 2027 (inferred). Roughly 1 in 5 EITC-eligible taxpayers don't claim it. The 2025 law (OBBBA/H.R. 1) ended the residential energy credits (25C, 25D) after Dec 31, 2025 and the new EV credit (30D) after Sept 30, 2025, so those are gone rather than "expiring soon."

### Cited Findings
**3-year refund window**
- The IRS estimated about $1.2 billion in unclaimed 2022 refunds owed to more than 1.3 million people, median refund $686. The deadline to file a 2022 Form 1040 and claim it was April 15, 2026 — [CPA Practice Advisor, Mar 23 2026](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/03/23/irs-owes-1-3-million-americans-about-686-each-deadline-to-claim-is-near/180074/); [Orbitax](https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/US-IRS-Announces-Deadline-fo-61520); [Fox 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/2022-tax-refund-april-15-deadline-looms)
- Taxpayers generally have three years to file and claim a refund. After that the money becomes property of the U.S. Treasury. Refunds can be held if later-year returns (2023, 2024) weren't filed, and can be offset for child support or federal debts. Most unclaimed refunds belong to people who never filed, often students and part-time workers below the filing threshold whose employers withheld tax — [Fox 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/2022-tax-refund-april-15-deadline-looms)
- A NATP blog (title) refers to "$12 billion in unclaimed refunds" at an earlier date (likely the COVID-era 2020 tax year). Treat it as context only. I did not verify the figure — [NATP](https://www.natptax.com/news-insights/blog/help-clients-claim-12-billion-in-unclaimed-refunds/)

**EITC**
- The IRS estimates about 1 in 5 eligible taxpayers don't claim the EITC. The maximum credit is cited as $7,830 (tax-year-2024 figure, 3+ children) — [CPA Practice Advisor, Feb 2025](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/02/03/the-biggest-tax-credit-many-people-miss-the-eitc/155391/)
- TIGTA: about 20% of eligible taxpayers don't claim about $7.3B of EITC each tax year (older report) — [TIGTA](https://www.tigta.gov/sites/default/files/reports/2024-11/2018IER004fr.pdf)
- The IRS national EITC participation rate for TY2022 is 80.8% — [IRS EITC participation rates by state](https://www.irs.gov/node/146421)
- The Census/IRS match for TY2021 found 78% participation by taxpayers and 81% by dollars (revised Feb 2026) — [Census CES-WP-24-75](https://www.census.gov/library/working-papers/2024/adrm/CES-WP-24-75.html)
- A dissenting academic study found non-claim rates at least 50–100% higher than official figures — [Brookings (2016)](https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/eitcparticipation.pdf)

**IRA / Roth IRA / HSA (deadline: tax filing day, about Apr 15, for the prior tax year)**
- 2026 IRA (traditional/Roth) limit is $7,500 (up from $7,000 in 2025); the 50+ catch-up is $1,100. Roth phase-out for 2026 is $153k–$168k (single/HoH) and $242k–$252k (MFJ) — [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/irs-ups-limits-for-401k-and-ira-contributions-in-2026); [Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/new-tax-change-could-mean-more-ira-and-401-k-savings)
- 2026 HSA limits are $4,400 self-only (from $4,300) and $8,750 family (from $8,550), with a $1,000 catch-up at 55+. HDHP minimum deductibles are $1,700/$3,400 — [CNBC via NBC](https://www.nbcmiami.com/news/business/money-report/irs-unveils-new-hsa-limits-for-2026-heres-what-investors-need-to-know/3605832/); [HR Morning](https://www.hrmorning.com/news/irs-announces-new-hsa-contribution-limits/)

**Saver's Credit**
- 2026 Saver's Credit AGI ceilings are $80,500 (MFJ), $60,375 (HoH) and $40,250 (single/MFS) — [Mutual of America](https://www.mutualofamerica.com/insights-and-tools/contribution-limits-2026); [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/irs-ups-limits-for-401k-and-ira-contributions-in-2026)

**Energy and EV credits ended by OBBBA (no longer claimable for new purchases)**
- 25C (energy-efficient home improvement): not allowed for property placed in service after Dec 31, 2025 — [IRS Energy Credit FAQs (FS-2025-05)](https://www.irs.gov/EnergyCreditFAQs); [IRS newsroom](https://www.irs.gov/vi/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-faqs-to-address-the-accelerated-termination-of-several-energy-provisions-under-obbb)
- 25D (residential clean energy, e.g. solar and batteries): not allowed for expenditures made after Dec 31, 2025 — same IRS source
- 30D (new clean vehicle), 25E (used EV) and 45W (commercial EV): not allowed for vehicles acquired after Sept 30, 2025. "Acquired" means a written binding contract plus a payment — same IRS source; [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/irs-provides-faqs-on-expiring-clean-energy-tax-breaks)

### Inferences
- The refund window currently open is tax year 2023, which closes about April 15, 2027. TY2022 closed April 15, 2026. A "file back returns" nudge to students who had W-2 withholding but never filed is high-value. Data source: W-2 box 2 (federal withholding); IRS online account / wage-and-income transcript.
- Energy credits are a "look-back" opportunity: someone who installed qualifying 2025 property and didn't claim 25C/25D on their 2025 return can still amend within the normal 3-year amendment window. Data source: contractor invoice, Form 5695.
- IRA/HSA prior-year contributions are allowed up to the filing deadline (about Apr 15, 2027 for TY2026). This is standard IRS practice but I did not re-verify it this session. Data source: W-2 (box 12 code W for HSA), brokerage 5498.
- A student with earned income and a Roth contribution under the AGI limit may also get the Saver's Credit. But full-time students are generally ineligible for the Saver's Credit (IRS rule, not verified this session). Flag this rather than promise it.

### Gaps
- **AOTC / LLC:** IRS pages returned 404 in this session. Unverified from memory, so confirm on IRS.gov: AOTC is up to $2,500 per student per year for the first 4 years, with 40% (up to $1,000) refundable, a MAGI phase-out of $80k–$90k single / $160k–$180k MFJ, and Form 1098-T generally required. LLC is up to $2,000 per return (20% of $10k), with no year limit and not refundable. OBBBA reportedly added an SSN requirement for AOTC (unverified). Both can be claimed retroactively by amending within 3 years. No IRS stat on unclaimed AOTC was found; GAO has reported many eligible filers don't claim education credits, but I found no source this session.
- **Saver's Match:** SECURE 2.0 replaces the Saver's Credit with a federal match deposited into a retirement account starting tax year 2027. Not confirmed this session.
- **State renter credits:** Examples from memory, unverified: California nonrefundable renter's credit ($60 single / $120 MFJ, filed with the CA return); Minnesota Renter's Credit (refundable, formerly the CRP form); Michigan/Wisconsin homestead credits. Not researched; deadline usually aligns with the state return or a later state-specific date.
- **529 state deductions:** Most states require the contribution by Dec 31; a few (e.g., IA, GA, MS, OK, SC, WI per common lists) allow until the state filing deadline. Not verified this session.
- **2026 EITC maximum** (inflation-adjusted figure beyond $7,830 for TY2024) not retrieved.

## Student aid: FAFSA/state grant deadlines, Pell rules and lifetime limits, unclaimed aid

### Takeaway
The high-school class of 2024 left about $4.4B in Pell Grants unclaimed by not filing the FAFSA, about 830k Pell-eligible students. The Cal Grant 2027-28 deadline is March 2, 2027, with no extension (FAFSA/CADAA opened Oct 1, 2026). Pell has a hard 600% (about 12 full-time semesters) lifetime cap. Starting 2026-27, OBBBA removes Pell for students whose SAI is at least 2x the max Pell or whose non-federal grants cover full cost of attendance, and adds Workforce Pell.

### Cited Findings
- NCAN: the class of 2024 left about $4.4B in Pell unclaimed (+$400M vs the class of 2023). About 830,000 Pell-eligible students didn't complete the FAFSA. FAFSA completion was 51.4% by Aug 30 vs 57.8% for the class of 2023, after the troubled FAFSA relaunch — [NASFAA](https://www.nasfaa.org/news-item/36182/NCAN_Unclaimed_Pell_Grants_Totaled_4_4_Billion_for_the_High_School_Class_of_2024)
- Earlier years: class of 2023 >$4B; class of 2022 about $3.6B (about 767k students); class of 2021 about $3.75B — [NASFAA 2022 report](https://nasfaa.org/news-item/29925/NCAN_Report_3_6_Billion_in_Pell_Grants_Went_Unclaimed_in_2022); [NASFAA 2021](https://www.nasfaa.org/news-item/26736/Study_Estimates_More_than_3_7_Billion_in_Aid_for_Pell_Grant-Eligible_Students_Went_Untapped); [ACE Higher Ed Today](https://www.higheredtoday.org/2025/05/05/pell-grant-dollars-are-left-unclaimed/)
- Cal Grant 2027-28 runs Oct 1, 2026 (FAFSA/CADAA open) to a March 2, 2027 priority deadline. Glendale lists the final date as June 30, 2028 (likely for other/CCC aid). A verified GPA must be submitted by the school to CSAC. The 2026-27 deadline was March 2, 2026 — [USC Financial Aid](https://financialaid.usc.edu/undergraduates/prospective/dates-and-deadlines.html); [Glendale CC](https://glendale.edu/financial-aid/deadlines/application-deadlines.html); [Palomar](https://www.palomar.edu/fa/event/2026-2027-deadline-for-calgrant-is-march-2-2026)
- CSAC reportedly said there is no extension or appeal for late filers; the 2025-26 extension was one-time because of the FAFSA disruption — [CollegeReadyParent (secondary)](https://collegereadyparent.org/downloads/CollegeReadyParent-California-Cheat-Sheet.pdf)
- Pell lifetime cap is 600% (6 years / 12 full-time semesters), counted across all schools. Each full-time semester equals 50%. Email notice comes at 450% and eligibility ends at 600%. Check under studentaid.gov, "My Aid," then Grants, then "Lifetime Eligibility Used" — [Wichita State](https://wichita.edu/administration/financial_aid/pell_leu.php); [ERAU](https://prescott.erau.edu/financial-aid/grants/pell-grant-leu); [SUU](https://suu.edu/finaid/lifetimepell.html)
- OBBBA Pell changes from 2026-27: students are ineligible if their SAI is at least 2x the max Pell or if non-federal grant aid covers the full COA. ED final rules came out May 1, 2026 — [Oregon State](https://financialaid.oregonstate.edu/legislative-updates); [Citizens Bank](https://www.citizensbank.com/student/articles/will-the-pell-grant-change-as-a-result-of-the-obbba.aspx)
- Workforce Pell covers short programs of about 150–599/600 clock hours (8–14 weeks) with 70% completion/placement benchmarks. It counts toward lifetime Pell and can't be combined with regular Pell. One school lists the effective date as July 1, 2026 — [Govst](https://www.govst.edu/fa-financial-aid-changes-2026-2027); [Govst FAQ](https://govst.edu/uploadedFiles/Admissions/Financial_Aid/Admissions_General/FAQ%20Document%20for%20OBBB.pdf); [Portico](https://welcome.porticoedu.com/obbba-resource-hub)

### Inferences
- The rescue action is "file the FAFSA/CADAA by the state priority date." Oct 1 open dates and early state deadlines (CA Mar 2) mean that missing by a day forfeits state grant dollars for the year. Data source: FAFSA Submission Summary (formerly SAR), state aid portal (CSAC WebGrants), school award letter.
- The new "full-COA scholarship = no Pell" rule means students should watch for Pell being reduced or cancelled after outside scholarships are reported. Data source: revised award letter.
- Work-study allocations not earned by term end are usually forfeited, since FWS is paid only as wages earned (standard federal rule, not verified this session). Data source: award letter and campus job portal.

### Gaps
- No source found this session for: national stats on unused Federal Work-Study; scholarship renewal deadlines (school-specific); student health insurance waiver deadlines (school-specific, typically before the first term's census date, e.g., UC SHIP waivers in summer/fall; not verified); tuition refund/withdrawal schedules (school-specific, usually 100% refund only through week 1–2); Return of Title IV 60% rule (standard, not verified this session); meal plan swipe resets (school-specific).
- No NCAN figure for the class of 2025 was found.

## Student perks lost at graduation

### Takeaway
Most student perks run on periodic re-verification through SheerID or school email, not a fixed graduation date, so they end at the first failed re-verification after graduation. Spotify Premium Student lasts up to 4 years with re-verification every 12 months, and auto-converts to full-price Premium. The GitHub Student Developer Pack requires current enrollment and is not a lifetime benefit.

### Cited Findings
- Spotify Premium Student: up to 4 years, re-verify every 12 months via SheerID. At the end it auto-switches to Premium Individual (signups before Nov 13, 2025) or Premium Standard (after) at full price unless cancelled — [Spotify Support](https://support.spotify.com/article/premium-student/)
- GitHub Student Developer Pack requires enrollment in a degree/diploma program, is not lifetime, and is checked through periodic re-verification. The status page is Settings, then Billing and plans, then Education benefits. Per a community post, new Copilot Student signups were paused at some point (date unclear); Copilot Free remains — [GitHub Education](https://education.github.com/pack); [GitHub community #111352](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/111352); [St. Cloud State KB](https://services.stcloudstate.edu/TDClient/1919/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=150289)
- A third-party source says Copilot via Student Pack is granted for 12 months at a time (secondary) — [fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/github-student-developer-pack-github-copilot-renewal.md)

### Inferences
- Rescue actions before graduation: export files and data from the .edu Google/Microsoft account, set forwarding (or note the alumni-email policy), redeem pending credits (AWS Educate / Azure for Students), and buy discounted hardware/software while still verifiable. Data source: university IT "after graduation" page, vendor account billing pages, calendar of graduation date.
- The auto-renew-to-full-price pattern (Spotify) is a common "silent price jump," useful for reminder apps.

### Gaps
- Not verified this session: Amazon Prime Student (commonly 6-month trial plus up to 4 years at a discount); Microsoft 365 A1/A3 loss after graduation (typically the school deprovisions accounts within weeks to months); Adobe student pricing (first-year promo then a price jump); Autodesk education (1-year renewable, requires verification); JetBrains (1-year renewable, plus a graduation discount offer); Apple Education pricing; Azure for Students ($100 credit, 12 months); .edu email retention periods (school-specific); campus gym and health center access ending at graduation or term end.

## Student loans: grace period, IDR/RAP, PSLF (status in 2026)

### Takeaway
2026 is a major transition year. A court vacated SAVE in March 2026. The new Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP) launched July 1, 2026 (1–10% of income, minus $50/month per dependent, forgiveness after 360 on-time payments). SAVE borrowers get a servicer notice and have 90 days to choose a plan or be auto-placed. New loans after July 1, 2026 can only use Standard or RAP. Grad PLUS ended for new borrowers July 1, 2026, with new grad, professional and Parent PLUS caps.

### Cited Findings
- SAVE was ended by court order (E.D. Mo. vacated the regulation) in March 2026. SAVE-forbearance months did not count toward PSLF or IDR forgiveness — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/student-loans/learn/save-lawsuits); [Protect Borrowers, May 2026](https://protectborrowers.org/so-much-for-saving-on-a-valuable-education-what-borrowers-need-to-know-now/); [studentaid.gov SAVE FAQ](https://studentaid.gov/articles/save/)
- From July 1, 2026 servicers contact SAVE borrowers. Borrowers who don't change plans within 90 days of the notice are auto-enrolled in another plan — [studentaid.gov SAVE FAQ](https://studentaid.gov/articles/save/)
- RAP: payments of 1–10% of income, reduced $50/month per dependent, with remaining balance forgiven after 360 qualifying monthly payments. It is available on the online IDR application — [ED fact sheet](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/fact-sheet-trump-administration-simplifying-student-loan-repayment); [College Investor](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/83324/borrowers-can-now-apply-for-the-new-rap-repayment-plan-online-at-studentaid-gov/)
- Loans first disbursed on or after July 1, 2026 are limited to RAP or Standard. The Tiered Standard plan does not count toward PSLF or IDR forgiveness. OBBBA removed the partial-financial-hardship requirement for IBR. PAYE/ICR close later; sources conflict on whether that is July 1, 2027 or July 1, 2028 — [Tate Law](https://www.tateesq.com/learn/income-driven-repayment-types); [Tate Law switch guide](https://go.tateesq.com/repayment/student-loan-repayment-save-switch-to-ibr/); [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/service-details/student-loan-assistance)
- Only Direct Loans qualify for PSLF. Consolidation can restart IDR forgiveness counts (per state guide) — [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/service-details/student-loan-assistance)
- Grad PLUS eliminated for new borrowers July 1, 2026; currently enrolled grad students in the same program keep access for up to 3 years. New caps: grad $20,500/yr and $100k aggregate; professional $50k/yr and $200k; Parent PLUS $20k/yr and $65k per student; overall $257,500 — [Oregon State](https://financialaid.oregonstate.edu/legislative-updates); [CCU](https://www.ccu.edu/financialaid/obbba-regulatory-changes/); [Henssler](https://www.henssler.com/key-tax-law-changes-to-higher-education-coming-soon); [Govst FAQ](https://govst.edu/uploadedFiles/Admissions/Financial_Aid/Admissions_General/FAQ%20Document%20for%20OBBB.pdf)

### Inferences
- For recent grads, the key deadline is the 6-month grace-period end (standard federal rule for Direct subsidized/unsubsidized loans, not re-verified this session). Choosing RAP or IBR before the first bill avoids default placement into Standard. Data source: studentaid.gov dashboard, servicer welcome letter, exit counseling confirmation.
- Annual IDR recertification still applies (RAP/IBR). Missing it can spike payments and capitalize interest. Data source: servicer recertification notice. PSLF: submit the employer certification form annually. Data source: PSLF Help Tool, employer HR.

### Gaps
- Exact current RAP recertification mechanics (auto IRS data sharing vs annual form) not confirmed.
- No 2026 stat found on borrowers missing the SAVE 90-day switch window.

## Benefits recertification: SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, ACA, unemployment

### Takeaway
Under H.R. 1/OBBBA, Medicaid expansion adults move to 6-month redeterminations and work requirements starting Jan 1, 2027 (some states, e.g., NJ, say Dec 31, 2026). SNAP work rules now cover ages 18–64 with a 3-months-in-36 limit and fewer exemptions. Enhanced ACA subsidies expired after 2025: premiums for subsidized enrollees rose about 114% on average, and 2026 sign-ups fell by over 1M.

### Cited Findings
- Medicaid: 6-month eligibility redeterminations for expansion adults start Jan 1, 2027 (CMS guidance March 2026; applies to full-expansion states). Mandatory work requirements must also be in place by Jan 1, 2027; states showing good faith can get extensions to Dec 31, 2028 — [Georgetown CCF](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/03/06/cms-releases-guidance-on-6-month-medicaid-renewals-for-expansion-adults/); [HFMA](https://www.hfma.org/payment-reimbursement-and-managed-care/medicaid-six-month-eligibility-redeterminations-cms-guidance/); [National Council timeline](https://hub.thenationalcouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/H.R.1_Implementation_Timeline_v2-2.pdf); [Physicians Practice, Sep 2026](https://www.physicianspractice.com/view/medicaid-work-requirements-will-land-on-the-front-desk-starting-jan-1); [MoneyTalksNews](https://www.moneytalksnews.com/medicaid-work-rules-begin-in-and-adults-to-could-be-hit-hardest/)
- Conflicting date: NJ says six-month checks begin Dec 31, 2026 — [ACNJ](https://acnj.org/new-medicaid-work-requirements-and-eligibility-redeterminations-starting-this-year/)
- SNAP: the ABAWD age range has expanded to 18–64. Recipients must work 20 hrs/wk (80/month) or are limited to 3 months of benefits in 36. The dependent-child exemption now applies only to children under 14. Exemptions for veterans, homeless people and former foster youth were removed — [SC DSS](https://dss.sc.gov/news/new-federal-snap-work-requirements-take-effect-in-south-carolina); [Hamilton Project](https://www.hamiltonproject.org/publication/paper/a-primer-on-snap-work-requirements/); [Hawaii DHS](https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/?p=14545)
- SNAP: half-time enrollment or 20 hrs/wk of class can count. Work-rule questions come at application or recertification — [VI DHS](https://dhs.vi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/VIDHS-Press-Release-SNAP-ABAWD-Work-Requirements-Effective-March-1-2026.pdf); [Hawaii DHS](https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/?p=14545)
- ACA: enhanced premium tax credits expired Dec 31, 2025, and eligibility returned to 100–400% FPL. Premium payments for subsidized enrollees rose about 114% on average, and 2026 plan selections were down by over 1M. 2026 open enrollment ended Dec 15 (Jan 1 coverage) and Jan 15 (Feb 1 coverage) — [KFF](https://kff.org/affordable-care-act/aca-marketplace-enrollment-is-down-in-2026-but-all-of-the-data-isnt-in-yet); [Families USA](https://www.familiesusa.org/resources/what-health-care-consumers-need-to-know-about-aca-marketplace-coverage-for-2026/)
- A House vote on a 3-year extension of enhanced credits was reported (17 Republicans joined Democrats). Enactment is not confirmed — [ACA Signups](https://acasignups.net/blogs/charles-gaba?page=13)

### Inferences
- Losing student health insurance or parental coverage at graduation or age 26 triggers a 60-day ACA Special Enrollment Period (standard rule, not re-verified this session). Missing it means waiting for open enrollment. Data source: SHIP termination letter, COBRA notice.
- Medicaid and SNAP losses are mostly procedural (missed renewal paperwork). The rescue action is responding to the agency renewal notice by its due date. Data source: state agency notice and benefits portal (e.g., BenefitsCal).

### Gaps
- 2027 ACA open enrollment dates (normally Nov 1–Jan 15 on HealthCare.gov) not verified; final status of the enhanced-subsidy extension unknown.
- KFF Medicaid unwinding totals (2023–24 disenrollments, share procedural) not retrieved this session; known from memory as about 25M disenrolled with about 69% procedural, but unverified.
- WIC recertification cadence and unemployment claim filing and weekly certification deadlines (state-specific) not researched.
- CBO figure on SNAP losses for older adults (about 800k) seen only on a low-quality blog; unverified.
