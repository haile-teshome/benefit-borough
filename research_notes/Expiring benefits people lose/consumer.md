# US Consumer Money-Back Windows and Expiring Value (excluding employment and credit cards)

Scope note: about 16 searches and fetches, as of 2026-10-24. Items marked "[background, unverified]" come from the researcher's prior knowledge, not a source fetched in this session. The report writer should treat them as low-confidence or leave them out.

## Gift cards, store credit, rewards points: federal 5-year rule, state rules, breakage totals

### Takeaway
Federal law (CARD Act, carried out through Regulation E) bars gift card funds from expiring sooner than 5 years after issuance or the last load. It also allows dormancy fees only after 12 months of non-use. Loyalty and promotional cards are not covered, so promo credit and points can expire much faster. Surveys repeatedly find that about 43-50% of US adults hold unused gift cards or store credit, worth roughly $21B-$27B in total, or about $167-$244 per person.

### Cited Findings
- Regulation E sets a minimum lifetime for gift card funds of 5 years from issuance, or 5 years from the last load. A dormancy, inactivity or service fee is allowed only after at least 12 months of non-use, and at most one such fee per calendar month. The rule covers store gift cards, merchant gift certificates and general-use prepaid gift cards. It does not cover loyalty, award or promotional cards. — [Federal Reserve 2010 final rule](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20100323a.htm); [Federal Register 75 FR 16580 (Apr 1, 2010)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2010-04-01/html/2010-6759.htm)
- States may add protections beyond the federal floor, such as cash-back requirements for small leftover balances. Where a state law is weaker, the federal 5-year rule still applies. — [OpenClassActions gift card laws by state](https://openclassactions.com/blog/gift-card-laws-by-state.php) (third-party guide; the state table could not be fully retrieved)
- Bankrate, Sept 2024 (fieldwork Aug 19-21, 2024): 43% of US adults hold at least one unused gift card, about $27B in total, averaging $244 per person. — [Bankrate press release PDF](https://bankrate.com/f/102997/x/c53caa68d4/gift-card-survey-press-release.pdf); [CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=110981)
- Bankrate, 2023: 47% hold at least one unused gift card or voucher, averaging $187, about $23B in total. — [Live5News/Gray (Aug 2023)](https://www.live5news.com/2023/08/24/nearly-half-americans-have-unused-gift-cards-survey-finds/); [AP via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/news/business/the-secret-life-of-gift-cards-heres-what-happens-to-the-billions-that-go-unspent-each-year/50828249)
- Bankrate, Feb 2020 (fieldwork Jan 15-17, 2020):
  - About $21B in unredeemed gift cards and store credits, averaging $167 per adult, with about half of adults holding some.
  - 25% of adults have let a gift card expire, and 22% have lost one.
  - 57% have held an unused card or credit for more than a year.
  - Households earning $80K or more averaged $297, against $93 for those under $40K.
  - The top reason was simply forgetting about the cards.
  - Source: [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/survey-americans-have-21-billion-in-unused-gift-card-balances/)
- CreditCards.com, 2022: average unused value of $175 per person. — [CreditCards.com 2022 survey PDF](https://Www.Creditcards.com/code/wp-content/uploads/20220822-gift-cards-survey.pdf)
- Breakage is the gift card liability that companies book as revenue because they expect it never to be redeemed, based on past patterns. Starbucks reported $212M of breakage revenue in 2022. In at least 19 states, including Delaware, unspent gift card value must go to state unclaimed property programs instead. — [AP via KSL, Dec 2023](https://www.ksl.com/article/news/business/the-secret-life-of-gift-cards-heres-what-happens-to-the-billions-that-go-unspent-each-year/50828249)
- NRF projected nearly $30B in holiday gift card sales (2023 season). This is a sales figure, not breakage. — [AP via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/news/business/the-secret-life-of-gift-cards-heres-what-happens-to-the-billions-that-go-unspent-each-year/50828249)

### Inferences
- Rescuing action: list every card and credit with its balance, spend or regift it, sell it on a resale site, or ask for cash-back where state law requires it.
- Where the data lives: physical cards, gift card emails, retailer accounts and wallets, and return receipts showing store credit or merchandise credit.
- Promotional "bonus" cards (for example, "$10 bonus card with a $50 purchase") are outside the 5-year rule and often expire within weeks. They are a high-loss category.
- Reported totals swing ($21B, $23B, $27B) because they are survey extrapolations. A reasonable headline is "$20B+ sitting unused at any time," not annual breakage.

### Gaps
- No authoritative current (2025/2026) national annual breakage dollar figure was found. Figures of about $3B/year are circulated, but none was verified this session.
- California cash-out rules (background: balances under $10 redeemable for cash) were not verified. Other state cash-out thresholds were also unverified.
- No sourced rewards-point expiration or forfeiture data (retail and airline points outside credit cards) was found.

## Return windows, price adjustment and price protection, warranties, purchase protection

### Takeaway
Major retailers stretch holiday return windows to mid or late January. For 2025 holiday purchases: Amazon Jan 31, 2026 (Apple products Jan 15), Walmart Jan 31, Target electronics Jan 24, Best Buy Jan 15, Macy's Jan 31. Restocking fees (Best Buy up to 15% on opened electronics) and receipt-less returns paid as store credit only cut into recovery. Sourced data on warranty claims, price adjustments and purchase protection was not obtained.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon: most items bought Nov 1 to Dec 31, 2025 can be returned until Jan 31, 2026. Apple-branded products have until Jan 15, 2026. — [About Amazon, holiday return policy 2025](https://aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-holiday-return-policy-2025)
- Retailer holiday windows reported by Money Talks News and Yahoo — [Money Talks News](https://www.moneytalksnews.com/retailers-with-extended-policies-for-the-holiday-season/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/amazon-target-walmart-big-retails-201300377.html):
  - Walmart: purchases Oct 1 to Dec 31, 2025 returnable until Jan 31, 2026. Not all Marketplace sellers participate.
  - Target: electronics and entertainment bought Nov 1 to Dec 24 returnable until Jan 24, 2026. Sources conflict on Apple, Beats and phones (one says Jan 8).
  - Best Buy: purchases Oct 31 to Dec 31, 2025 returnable until Jan 15, 2026. Opened electronics carry restocking fees of up to 15%.
  - Macy's: purchases Oct 6 to Dec 31, 2025 returnable until Jan 31, 2026.
- Without a receipt, retailers generally give store credit or an exchange rather than a refund. — [Yahoo Shopping](https://shopping.yahoo.com/gift-ideas/articles/returning-gifts-heres-return-policies-211520194.html)

### Inferences
- Rescuing action: put each purchase's last return date on a calendar. Keep the order email or receipt, and the gift receipt for gifts.
- Where the data lives: order confirmation emails and retailer order history.
- Standard non-holiday windows are much shorter than holiday windows. [background, unverified: commonly 14-30 days for electronics and 30-90 days for general merchandise]

### Gaps
- No sourced figures were found for price-adjustment or price-match policies at major retailers. Many retailers dropped post-purchase price adjustments, but this was not verified.
- Manufacturer warranty registration and claim rates, and extended warranty (service contract) claim rates, are unsourced. [background, unverified: industry claims show service contracts have low claim rates and high margins]
- Card purchase protection (90-120 days) and extended warranty benefits: out of scope (credit cards). Not researched.

## Free trials, auto-renewals, cancellation windows; FTC click-to-cancel status

### Takeaway
The FTC's 2024 click-to-cancel rule (amending the Negative Option Rule) was vacated by the 8th Circuit on July 8, 2025, days before its main compliance date. In March 2026 the FTC issued an Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (ANPRM) to revisit it, which is a years-long process. As of late 2026, consumers rely on ROSCA, FTC Act Section 5 and state automatic-renewal laws.

### Cited Findings
- The FTC finalized the click-to-cancel rule in Oct 2024. Its main provisions were due to take effect in 2025. — [ABC7/ABC News](https://abc7.com/post/ftc-adopts-click-cancel-rule-aiming-make-easier-memberships/15437401/)
- Custom Communications, Inc. v. FTC, No. 24-3137 (8th Cir. July 8, 2025) vacated the whole rule. The court held the FTC violated FTC Act Section 22 by skipping a preliminary regulatory analysis even though the rule's annual costs exceeded $100M. — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eighth-circuit-vacates-ftc-click-to-cancel-rule-days-before-compliance-deadline); [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/es/insights/2025/7/eighth-circuit-vacates-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule); [Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/zh-hans/insights/newsupdates/2025/07/us-ftc-click-to-cancel-rule-struck-down)
- The laws still in force are ROSCA, FTC Act Section 5, state automatic-renewal laws and state UDAP (unfair and deceptive practices) laws. — [Dickinson Wright](https://www.dickinson-wright.com/news-alerts/rule-interrupted-click-to-cancel-is-click-to-gone)
- In March 2026 the FTC issued an ANPRM on the Negative Option Rule. It asks whether to readopt provisions of the vacated 2024 rule. The analysis calls this the start of a years-long process. — [BCLP via JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/i-m-not-dead-yet-is-the-demise-of-the-1032323/)

### Inferences
- Rescuing action: when you start a trial, set a reminder about 2 days before it converts. Check annual renewal dates. Cancel online where state law requires it.
- Where the data lives: the sign-up confirmation email, renewal-notice emails (many state laws require pre-renewal notice for annual plans), and app-store subscription lists.

### Gaps
- No sourced statistics were found on the share of trials that convert unintentionally, or on dollars wasted on unused subscriptions. Survey figures exist (for example, from CNET/C+R Research) but were not verified this session.
- No state-by-state list of automatic-renewal laws (such as California's ARL amendments effective July 2025) was verified.

## Mail-in rebates, class action settlements, unclaimed property

### Takeaway
Mail-in rebates make money because consumers fail to redeem them. Traceable estimates range from 1-2% redemption on some offers to around 50%, and forgetting is the top reason. In consumer class actions the FTC's study found a median claims rate of 9% (weighted mean 4%). Megasettlements pay about $30 per claimant. Unclaimed property is the largest pool: about $70B held by states, with only about $4-4.5B returned per year.

### Cited Findings
**Rebates**
- Prof. Sridhar Moorthy (University of Toronto) found redemption of 1-2% on the rebates he studied, with a maximum of about 50%. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/eradicate-rebates/)
- ConsumerAffairs data puts redemption at 5-80% depending on rebate value. Forgetting to submit was the top reason for not redeeming, at 41%. — [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/cms/customer-experience/mailin-rebates-annoying-customers-one-upc-code-at-a-time-024746.php) (secondary)
- A University of Florida study of more than 1,200 people found redemption was higher with shorter deadlines (1 day versus 3 weeks), so longer windows feed procrastination. — [UF News 2004](https://archive.news.ufl.edu/articles/2004/06/redemption-of-mail-in-rebates-declines-with-increased-time-allowance.html)
- Non-redemption of software rebates has been reported as high as 90% (Weitz & Wensley, Handbook of Marketing). — [PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/mail_in_rebates-2495678203.html) (secondary)

**Class action settlements**
- FTC staff report "Consumers and Class Actions" (Sept 2019), covering 149 consumer settlements:
  - Median claims rate of 9%, weighted mean 4%.
  - Mailed claim packets: median about 16%, weighted mean 10%. Email notice was lowest (median 3%, mean 2%).
  - Requiring documentary proof cut claims by about 90%.
  - Median claim approval rate was 93%.
  - Sources: [Ballard Spahr](https://ballardspahr.com/insights/alerts-and-articles/2019/10/ftc-study-class-action-settlement-notices-have-room-to-improve); [Carlton Fields](https://www.carltonfields.com/insights/blogs/classified-the-class-action-blog/migrated/take-notice-ftc-reports-on-claims-rates-and-settlement-notices)
- Facebook $725M privacy settlement:
  - About 28M claims were filed, a likely US record, and about 17M were deemed eligible.
  - Payouts began Sept 3, 2026, spread over 10 weeks.
  - The average payment was about $29.43, the maximum $38.36.
  - Attorneys received $181M.
  - Sources: [NBC Philadelphia](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/news/national-international/725m-facebook-settlement-payments-have-started-to-go-out-when-can-you-expect-yours/4268081/); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/facebook-privacy-settlement-payments-payout-user-2025-legit-email)
  - Caveat: the September date was reported in search results and fits the article dates. Verify before citing.

**Unclaimed property**
- States returned more than $4.49B to owners in FY2024 (July 2023 to June 2024). States hold roughly $70B in total, per NAUPA. Earlier reporting put the average claimed asset at $2,080. — [Bitcoin.com News citing NAUPA](https://news.bitcoin.com/americans-are-sitting-on-20-billion-they-dont-know-they-own-heres-how-to-check-in-2-minutes-49201/); [Fox LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/theres-billion-dollars-unclaimed-money-us-check-see-youre-owed-money)
- The NAUPA FY25 report (July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025; 49 member offices reporting) shows a headline figure of $4,252,648,747. It is most likely dollars returned, but the label could not be confirmed because the PDF is image-based. — [NAUPA FY25 Report](https://unclaimed.org/wp-content/uploads/NAUPA-FY-25-Report.pdf)
- An older Stateline/NAUPA figure: $7.8B collected against $3.2B returned in one year, so far more comes in than goes back out. The year was unclear. — [search snippet, Stateline via Fox10](https://fox10phoenix.com/news/theres-billion-dollars-unclaimed-money-us-check-see-youre-owed-money)
- State examples:
  - New York returned a record $633M in FY2024-25, up 25%. — [NY OSC](https://www.osc.ny.gov/unclaimed-funds)
  - Pennsylvania returned a record $334.1M in 2025 and still holds more than $5B. — [MyChesCo](https://www.mychesco.com/?p=656701)
  - Connecticut returned a record $120.9M in FY2025. — [CT Treasurer](https://portal.ct.gov/ott/unclaimed-property/year-in-review)
  - Washington returned a record $154M in FY24. — [WA DOR report](https://dor.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/UCPAnnualReport.pdf)
  - Wyoming returned $23.76M through 7,621 payments in FY2026. — [KOTA](https://www.kotatv.com/2026/07/07/wyoming-unclaimed-property-division-reunites-owners-with-24m-largest-claim-near-977k/)
- In at least 19 states, unspent gift card balances escheat (pass) to the state unclaimed property program. — [AP via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/news/business/the-secret-life-of-gift-cards-heres-what-happens-to-the-billions-that-go-unspent-each-year/50828249)

### Inferences
- Unclaimed property does not truly expire, since states hold it indefinitely. But the return rate of about $4.3-4.5B a year against about $70B held suggests roughly 6% of the stock is reclaimed annually. It is the largest "money you are owed but haven't claimed" pool.
- Where the data lives: the unclaimed property database for each state where you have lived (MissingMoney.com searches most participating states at once), plus old addresses and maiden names.
- Settlements: the claim deadline is in the settlement notice (email or postcard) and on the settlement administrator's website. The FTC refunds page lists FTC-administered refunds, which are usually sent automatically. ClassAction.org and Top Class Actions list open settlements with deadlines.
- Because claim rates are low, claimants in no-proof settlements often get more than the advertised estimate, since unclaimed funds are redistributed pro rata.
- Rebates: photograph the UPC, receipt and submission. Rebate deadlines are often 30 days after purchase. Rebate debit cards themselves often expire in 6-12 months, which compounds the loss. [background, unverified]

### Gaps
- No verified, labeled NAUPA FY2025 national total was obtained (see the caveat above). There is also no verified national count of claims paid.
- No recent (post-2015) rigorous industry study of mail-in rebate redemption was found.
- The FTC's annual refunds data was not fetched. [background, unverified: FTC actions return several hundred million dollars per year]

## Insurance, utility and energy rebates, airline and hotel refunds

### Takeaway
Under DOT's automatic refund rule (effective Oct 28, 2024), airlines must refund cancelled or significantly changed flights within 7 days, to the original payment method. "Significant" means more than 3 hours domestic or 6 hours international, and the passenger must not have accepted rebooking or credit. Checked-bag fees are refunded if a domestic bag is more than 12 hours late. On energy, the OBBBA (Public Law 119-21) ended the 25C and 25D homeowner credits for 2025 and earlier, and 30D for vehicles acquired after Sept 30, 2025. IRA HOMES and HEAR rebates continue state by state through Sept 30, 2031, or until funds run out.

### Cited Findings
- DOT refund rule:
  - Effective Oct 28, 2024. Refunds are automatic and prompt unless the passenger chooses other compensation.
  - "Significant change" means more than 3 hours domestic or more than 6 hours international.
  - Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 days, not as miles unless the passenger requests it.
  - Bag fees are refunded if the bag isn't delivered within 12 hours of a domestic arrival and the passenger filed a mishandled-baggage report.
  - Sources: [Checkbook.org](https://www.checkbook.org/national/consumers-notebook/articles/New-Rule-Requires-Airlines-to-Provide-Automatic-Refunds-for-Canceled-or-Delayed-Flights-7739); [Fox5 Vegas](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2024/04/24/airlines-will-be-forced-issue-cash-refunds-cancellations-significant-delays-under-new-dot-rule)
- IRS FS-2025-05 (Aug 21, 2025) on OBBBA terminations:
  - 25C: not allowed for property placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.
  - 25D: not allowed for expenditures after Dec 31, 2025.
  - 30D: ends for vehicles acquired after Sept 30, 2025.
  - Sources: [IRS newsroom FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/vi/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-faqs-to-address-the-accelerated-termination-of-several-energy-provisions-under-obbb); [Dean Dorton](https://deandorton.com/articles/obbba-insight-termination-of-energy-credits/)
- IRA HOMES and HEAR rebates run until funding is exhausted or Sept 30, 2031. Rollout is uneven by state and requires DOE approval of each state's application and Blueprint. A 2025 federal pause delayed disbursement. — [NC Clean Energy Tech Center 50-state tracker](https://nccleantech.ncsu.edu/?p=12985); [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/states-energy-efficiency-rebates-inflation-reduction-act-doe-trump/756981/); [Hawaii State Energy Office](https://energy.hawaii.gov/ehale-rebates/)

### Inferences
- 25C and 25D can still be claimed on 2025 tax returns, filed in 2026, for qualifying 2025 installs. That claim is the remaining window. Where the data lives: contractor invoices and manufacturer certification statements.
- Utility rebates (thermostat, HVAC, appliance) usually require applying within 60-90 days of purchase. [background, unverified]
- Airline: even with automatic refunds, travelers who accepted a voucher, or whose flights changed only modestly, must track credit expiration dates. Where the data lives: the airline confirmation email and the "trip credits" page in the airline account.
- Insurance: cancelling a policy midterm usually yields a pro-rata refund of unearned premium. Many people keep duplicate coverage, such as roadside assistance through auto insurance, AAA and a credit card. [background, unverified]

### Gaps
- No sourced data was found on unused insurance perks, refunds of unearned premium, or roadside duplication.
- Hotel refund rights: no federal rule identified. The FTC junk-fee rule for short-term lodging and live-event tickets (effective May 2025) was not verified this session.
- Whether DOT under the 2025-26 administration changed or waived parts of the refund rule was not verified. (DOT did roll back a separate proposed compensation rule; also unverified.)
- HSA-like items are outside this scope and likely covered by the employment researcher. Not researched. Note that FSA use-it-or-lose-it is employer-linked.
